An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a guide in our own words that cites its sources by name, a roadmap to the platinum, missable trophies flagged from the official descriptions, and a checklist that saves progress in the browser with export and import, filters by grade and kind, search, and a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until they are earned. It runs entirely from its own files, with no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
